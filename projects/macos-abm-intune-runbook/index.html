macOS ABM to Intune Enterprise Runbook

Personal-lab portfolio project · exact claim boundaries and direct retained proof

ImplementationTroubleshootingValidation and proof

Objective and implementation

Document an enterprise macOS management sequence while separating actual Intune configuration from simulated endpoint work. Technologies: Intune, APNs, macOS compliance, FileVault settings, ABM/ADE planning, and capability review.

The matrix records APNs creation/upload and macOS compliance and FileVault policy objects as real configuration. The separate scenario document describes the intended ABM-to-Intune lifecycle as simulated.

Troubleshooting and evidence interpretation

A configuration surface demonstrates capability availability; a saved policy object demonstrates configuration. Neither establishes endpoint execution. No enrolled Mac is supplied for FileVault escrow, app installation, Platform SSO registration, certificate issuance, compliance evaluation, or destructive lifecycle validation.

Scope and limitations

Real configuration includes APNs, macOS compliance, FileVault Settings Catalog, enrollment restriction review, and capability review. ABM organization, ADE assignment/enrollment, managed-Mac Platform SSO, live PKI/SCEP issuance, RADIUS auth, FileVault escrow from a managed Mac, managed-Mac compliance evaluation, app installation, and destructive lifecycle actions were not performed and must not be claimed as implemented.

Registry status retained in the audit: COMPLETE — with explicit real-vs-simulated boundaries. . Evidence below is assessed by artifact type; this package does not rerun the lab.
