Designed and documented an enterprise macOS management runbook spanning Apple Business Manager, Microsoft Intune, Automated Device Enrollment, Microsoft Entra authentication, Platform SSO, FileVault, compliance, enterprise Wi-Fi, certificates, application deployment, inventory, and device lifecycle administration. ## LinkedIn project description

Created an enterprise macOS management project focused on accurate operational design rather than fabricated completeness. In a real Microsoft Intune tenant, I configured an Apple MDM Push certificate, created a macOS compliance baseline, built a FileVault Settings Catalog profile, and validated available Platform SSO, enterprise Wi-Fi, certificate, app-deployment, inventory, and lifecycle controls. Because I do not have an Apple Business Manager organization or managed Mac in this lab, ADE, ABM token exchange, endpoint Platform SSO, FileVault escrow, and destructive lifecycle operations are clearly documented as simulated procedures instead of being presented as completed production work.

## Interview explanation

> I wanted the project to show both technical knowledge and administrative judgment. I used the real Intune tenant for everything I could legitimately configure, including APNs, compliance, and FileVault. I also inspected the real Platform SSO, Wi-Fi, SCEP, app, inventory, and lifecycle surfaces. The Apple Business Manager and ADE portions are documented as simulations because I do not have an ABM organization or enrolled Mac. That boundary matters because creating an Intune policy is not the same as proving endpoint enforcement.
