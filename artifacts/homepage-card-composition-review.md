# Homepage Card Composition Correction

Visual review of the deployed homepage identified two composition problems:

- Target-role cards used alternating vertical offsets that read as accidental misalignment.
- The flagship evidence card placed its content at the bottom of an oversized empty surface.

The correction aligns the target-role cards and converts the flagship evidence card into a compact inspection surface with top-aligned content and a restrained system-path treatment.

No public claim, proof link, evidence classification, metric, metadata value, navigation target, or limitation statement is changed.
