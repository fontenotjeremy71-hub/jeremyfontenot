Proxmox Isolated Restore Validation — Linux, Windows, Active Directory, and pfSense

The completed cycle used four temporary VMs, preboot network isolation, bounded local validation, checksum-backed evidence, clean shutdown, and verified cleanup. Production VMs 100, 200, 300, and 400 remained protected.

Linux01 · PASS

Scoped Linux restore objectives passed.

The selected archive restored, booted, supported local OS, disk, disconnected-interface, and service-state checks, then shut down and cleaned up successfully.

WS01 · INCONCLUSIVE

Boot passed; in-guest validation was not tested.

No authorized local Windows account was available. The visible domain account was not used, and no access modification was attempted.

DC01 · INCONCLUSIVE

Selected service checks passed; LDAP-dependent checks remained inconclusive.

Elevated NTDS, core-service, SYSVOL, and NETLOGON checks passed. Local LDAP/RootDSE, localhost AD cmdlets, and LDAP-dependent dcdiag did not complete while isolation remained enforced.

pfSense · Narrow PASS

Archive, isolated boot, local observations, and cleanup passed.

Live WAN/LAN, routing, NAT, firewall-policy, DHCP-client, DNS-forwarding, VPN, identity/certificate, application, and business-service recovery were not tested.

Claim Boundary

Recovery timing and full disaster recovery remain unproven.

RTO: NOT MEASURED. RPO: NOT MEASURED. Full disaster recovery: NOT PROVEN. PASS describes only directly validated isolated objectives.
